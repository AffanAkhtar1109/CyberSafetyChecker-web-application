Safety tips

  • Never share OTPs or passwords. Real banks and companies do not ask for them by message or call.
  • Do not click suspicious links. Type the official website address yourself instead.
  • Verify the sender. Check the email address or number, and contact the company on its official channel.
  • Use strong passwords. Make them long and unique for every account. A password manager helps.
  • Enable two-factor authentication. It protects your account even if a password leaks.
  • Pause when you feel pressure. Urgency and fear are the scammer's main tools.

Cyber Safety Checker is an educational project. It does not send your text anywhere: all checks run in your browser.
